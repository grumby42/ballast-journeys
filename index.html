Be the aeronaut

Real journeys, flown again. You take the basket, and sometimes the ground crew's place, on the actual day, with the real balloon's size, gas and ballast. As you fly, the real records arrive: the pilots' own logs, letters from people watching, families waiting for news. Keep your own log; at the end it sits beside theirs. The physics is real. The story never goes beyond the record: anything the game had to simplify or infer is labelled.

How the record is used

Every entry from the record is a short digest in this game's own words, with its source. Direct quotations come only from public-domain originals: texts published before 1929, or nineteenth-century letters and telegrams by writers long dead. Foreign-language quotes are given in the original with a translation. Modern translations and modern books are never quoted. Each story beat carries a label: documented means it's in the record; inferred means it follows from the record but isn't stated; simplified means the game changed something to make it playable, and says what.
